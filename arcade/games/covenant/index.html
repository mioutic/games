Press Turn

Each side opens its phase with one icon per living combatant. Icons are spent, not taken in order — so a phase can be one action or eight.

Ordinary action1 icon
Weakness or critical½ icon
Miss or evade2 icons
Blocked (null)2 icons
Repelled or drainedphase ends
Guard / pass½ icon

A half icon is spent before a full one, except by a weakness or a critical — which is what lets a good read chain. The enemy plays by the same rules, so your own weaknesses hand them the same gift.

❦

Negotiation

Talk instead of fighting. A demon's mood decides which register of answer it wants — kind, serious, playful or evasive. Read the mood, not the question. They will also ask for macca, or for your own blood. Demons above your level will not serve you, and a full party leaves nowhere to put them.

❦

Party

Tap a demon to summon or send it back to stock. Four in the field, counting you.

❦

Fusion

Pick two. What comes out is the average of their levels, one race up, and it keeps what it can of their skills.
