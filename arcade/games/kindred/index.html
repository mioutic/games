Kindred

You are born into a village at the edge of the cold. One minute is one year; at sixty you are gone, and someone you raised carries on.

As a baby you can say one letter. Say F and your mother may come. Grow, learn the old ways from the elders, keep the fire, feed the small ones. The people nearby hear what you say and answer in their own words.

Tap the ground to walk, tap a thing to use what you hold on it. Hold on a basket to take from it. Drag in the lower left to steer.

Click to walk or to use what you hold on a thing; right-click a basket to take from it. WASD to walk, Enter to speak, Space to eat or wear, Q to drop, the wheel to zoom, F11 for full screen.
